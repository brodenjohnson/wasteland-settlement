// ---------------------------------------------------------------- ECHOES FROM THE CASE
=== echo_The Long Table
~ A drafting table under a green lamp. Your hand, ruling lines. Twelve beds. Then forty. Then two hundred.
~ Every stage has a box beside it that says WATER and a box that says FOOD, and you will not let yourself draw the next stage until both boxes are filled in.
~ Somebody asks how long it will take. You say: as long as it takes. They do not like that answer. You did not expect them to.

=== echo_Minutes of the Vote
~ A long room with no windows and good chairs. Paper being passed down a table, hand to hand, and nobody reading it.
~ A man at the end says "all those in favour" in the voice of someone who has already counted.
~ Hands go up. You are watching your own hand to see what it does. You do not remember what it does.

=== echo_Director Code Card
~ A card being pressed into your palm. A woman's voice, tired and exact: "Don't carry it with the roster. Promise me."
~ You promise. You are already wondering why.

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- THE CANTOR ON THE RADIO
=== cantor_sermon_1
~ Your tag crackles against your wrist. Not the call sign this time. A man's voice, warm, unhurried, the voice of somebody who used to read the weather.
Cantor: Good evening, listeners. Eleven tags, one answering. We have all heard it. Some of us have heard it every night for a very long time.
Cantor: The voice under the sea is calling its family home. I only ask the obvious question. Why only eleven?
~ The signal folds back into static.

=== cantor_sermon_2
~ The tag again. The same warm voice.
Cantor: A door that opens for some is not a door. It is a wall with a guest list.
Cantor: Three thousand sleeping under the breakwater. Somebody chose them. Somebody chose us, too. We were chosen to be outside.
Cantor: I would like to meet whoever does the choosing. I think they are walking about. I think they have been to the door.

=== cantor_sermon_3
~ The tag. He sounds closer tonight, which is not possible, and you check anyway.
Cantor: To the one who carries the eleventh tag. I know you can hear me. You stood at the door and you did not open it.
Cantor: That was a choice. Everything is a choice. Come to Chapel Row and tell us why you made it. We will listen. Listening is what we do.

=== cantor_sermon_4
~ The tag. Only a few words this time, very gently.
Cantor: For everyone, or for no one. Goodnight, listeners.
