The four bars

  • Health. You heal slowly on your own while you are fed and watered. Bandages, medkits and some food heal faster.
  • Hunger and thirst drain all the time. At zero you start losing health. Thirst drains faster than hunger.
  • Radiation builds up out in the Wastes, faster in ruins and on the shore, and from drinking dirty water. While it is high it eats your health. The ground around your bedroll is clean and slowly draws it back out of you.

Water

Open water fills you up but gives you a good dose of radiation. The spring in Home Valley is cleaner. A Water Purifier has to be built right beside water and fills empty bottles with purified water, which is close to clean. Purified water also feeds sprinklers through pipes (see irrigation).

Healing and radiation

The Hull Clinic will patch you up for 25 caps or clear your radiation for 35. See The Hull.

Meal buffs

Cooked meals give buffs for a while as well as filling you up. The Cooking skill makes them last longer. Times are in real seconds.

Difficulty

Change it any time in Settings. Percentages are against Normal.
