A container ship that ran aground in the marsh and was never refloated. People moved into the boxes. Then they built a town around it.

Getting there

The Hull sits in the far south-east corner of the Wastes, past the Toxic Marsh. Follow the road south-east from the marsh until it runs out at the gangway. Reach the marsh and it goes on your map, and travelling merchants will tell you about it if you ask about the road.

Nothing attacks you in town. Every building is its own place behind a door. The town has streetlamps and gets properly dark after 21:00.

Living there

  • Everyone has a routine. 37 people live here, each with a job, a workplace, a bed and a daily routine on the game clock. Shops open at 8:00 and close at 18:00. People drink at the Rusty Anchor in the evening and sleep from about 22:00 to 6:00. Walk into a house at night and they will be asleep, and will not talk until morning.
  • Trade. Ten vendors, each with their own stock that changes daily. Each pays extra for the kind of thing they deal in: the stockyard for farm goods, the scrapyard for materials, and so on.
  • Hull reputation. Doing jobs from the job board raises it. Each point takes 2% off what vendors charge you and adds 2% to what they pay. Higher reputation also opens better stock.
  • Services. The doctor patches you up for 25 caps or clears radiation for 35. The inn rents a guest bunk for 15 caps and you sleep until morning. The barkeep (10 caps) and the Reclaimers (20 caps) sell rumours that mark places on your map. Drifters can be hired as settlers for 40 caps if you have a free bed at home.

Buildings

There are also eight cargo homes along the cargo row. The Archive and the Pit are shuttered for now.
