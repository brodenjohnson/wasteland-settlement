Ten people woke in the ruins around the same time you did, each with a numbered brass tag on their wrist and no clear memory of how it got there. You will find them scattered across the Wastes. Each one has a favour to ask. Help them and they will join your settlement if you have a bed for them, and they get better at their work once their story is done.

What they remember, and what it means, is for you to find out in the game.

Other people

Travelling merchants, travellers with rumours, wounded strangers and the occasional drifter all turn up on the roads. Some will join you. Some want something. How you treat them is remembered, and your journal keeps a page of the choices that mattered. The people of the Hull are listed on The Hull page.
