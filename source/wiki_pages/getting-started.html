The first day

You wake in the Wastes with nothing but the clothes you are in and a note in your pocket. Read it (open your bag with I and right-click it). Then get a roof over your head before dark.

  1. Pick a spot for your camp. Home Valley has a spring and trees and is the obvious choice, but you can settle anywhere. Wherever you put your Bedroll becomes your camp, and the ground around it is clear of radiation. See Your settlement.
  2. Gather. Click dead trees for wood and scrap piles for scrap. Your fists will do at first. A Rusty Hatchet and a Rusty Pick are much faster.
  3. Build a Workbench and a Campfire. Open crafting with C. The Workbench makes tools and buildings. Build a Weapon Bench, Armour Bench and Medical Station too: weapons, armour and medicine are made at those, standing next to them. The campfire cooks.
  4. Find water. Drinking from open water fills you up but gives you radiation. A Water Purifier built right beside water gives you clean bottles. See Survival.
  5. Plant something. A Hoe tills soil. Seeds go in tilled soil and need watering each day. See Farming.
  6. Sleep. Click your bedroll to sleep. At night you sleep through to morning, which skips the dangerous hours. You can nap in the day too, and Space gets you up.

Your journal (J) lists your current goals, step by step. The first few teach you the basics. See Quests.

Saving

Each character has an autosave and three save slots. The game autosaves each morning and when you sleep (you can turn this off in Settings). Save by hand from the Esc menu.

Saves live in your browser. Clearing your browser data deletes them, and they do not follow you to another computer. To keep a copy, use Export on the Characters screen, or Export this character in the save menu. It downloads one file with every slot in it. Import on the Characters screen brings it back. Importing never overwrites a character: if you already have them, you get a second copy.

Export before you clear your browser, change computers or try something risky.

When you die

You wake at your bedroll. Everything you were carrying and wearing stays with your body where you fell, and a marker shows you where. Go back and get it. If you die again before you do, the first stash is gone for good.

Settings worth knowing

  • Difficulty changes hunger, thirst, radiation, raid size and enemy toughness together. See the table.
  • When a settler falls: by default a settler who drops is knocked out for a day and then comes back. You can switch on permanent death.
  • Gore: Full, Some or Off. Text size: Normal, Large or Larger. Pause in menus, screen shake, the low-health effect and the washed-out palette can all be turned off.

The game is for adults. It has graphic violence and gore, and the gore can be turned down or off at any time.
