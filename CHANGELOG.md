# Changelog

## 0.9.1
- Export and import characters. Characters and saves has Import and Export buttons, and the in-game save and load panels have "Export this character". The file holds every save slot. Importing never overwrites: a character already in this browser comes in as a copy.
- Fixed: the Quorum quest could stay open after reading the console if you reached the Breakwater without passing Chapel Row. Affected saves fix themselves on load.

## 0.9.0
- The Hull: a town of 37 residents with jobs, homes and daily routines, 15 enterable buildings, ten vendors, a clinic, an inn and a job board.
- Livestock: twohead cows and roach hens, feeding, breeding, sickness and the Beast Tonic.
- Arsenal: 29 weapons with their own art, three new wastelander armour sets, weapon and armour benches, skulls and pikes.
- The Tidewrack: tides, fog, Odile, the Listeners, the Wrack Mother. Chapter Two playable.
- Crops by tier, farming and cooking perks, irrigation with buried pipes and sprinklers.
- Balance pass on bosses, enemy levels, crop values and prices.
