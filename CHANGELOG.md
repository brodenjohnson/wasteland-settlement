# Changelog

## 0.10.0
- Chapter Three: The Long Table. Go home and ask Marta what is in the case. Four new quests, a new boss, the Cantor's voice on the radio at night, and a decision at a long table that Chapter Four will remember.
- The settlement panel shows how many people your valley could take in, and what is holding the number down.
- Settlers can still only die if you turn that on in Settings.

## 0.9.3
- The Hull is safe ground. Nothing ambushes you in town.
- The Hull has a lot more life in it: rubble and rust along the ship, crates and planters outside the shops, market stalls in the plaza, washing lines between the homes, drum fires that burn at night, a working dock, and grime on every street.
- Fixed: you could not move after walking into Tinkers' Guildhall, Council Hall, Harbour Guard HQ or the Reclaimers' Lodge.
- The Hull now sits in the far south-east corner, at the end of a road that runs past the marsh. Reaching the marsh puts it on your map, and travelling merchants will tell you about it.
- MegaMart moved to the east edge of Fallen City, and Pylon 3 to the north of the Ash Tower, each at the end of a road. Existing saves move them automatically.
- Rumours can now point you at the places under the Wastes.
- Hollowclaw Hide is worth something now.
- The wiki has a picture for every item and creature, says which region each survivor is in, and is now actually online.

## 0.9.2
- A player wiki, linked from the title screen: getting started, survival, the settlement, skills, every recipe, weapon, armour piece and item, farming, cooking and livestock, creatures and bosses, places, the Hull and its people, the ten survivors and quests. Numbers come straight from the game. No story spoilers.
- Fixed: the Armoury and the Rusty Anchor in the Hull could not build their stock, so trading with them failed.

## 0.9.1
- Export and import characters. Characters and saves has Import and Export buttons, and the in-game save and load panels have "Export this character". The file holds every save slot. Importing never overwrites: a character already in this browser comes in as a copy.
- Fixed: the Quorum quest could stay open after reading the console if you reached the Breakwater without passing Chapel Row. Affected saves fix themselves on load.

## 0.9.0
- The Hull: a town of 37 residents with jobs, homes and daily routines, 15 enterable buildings, ten vendors, a clinic, an inn and a job board.
- Livestock: twohead cows and roach hens, feeding, breeding, sickness and the Beast Tonic.
- Arsenal: 29 weapons with their own art, three new wastelander armour sets, weapon and armour benches, skulls and pikes.
- The Tidewrack: tides, fog, Odile, the Listeners, the Wrack Mother. Chapter Two playable.
- Crops by tier, farming and cooking perks, irrigation with buried pipes and sprinklers.
- Balance pass on bosses, enemy levels, crop values and prices.
