Your settlement

The safe zone, camp levels, settlers and their jobs, and raids.

The safe zone

Your Bedroll is the heart of your camp. The ground around it is free of radiation, and that zone grows as your camp levels up. Raids come for your camp, wherever it is. Benches (the Workbench, Weapon Bench and Armour Bench) work from anywhere inside the zone, so you do not have to stand next to them.

Camp levels

Your camp earns points for the people in it and what it is built from, and levels up when the points reach the next step. The settlement panel (B) shows your level and points, for example Level 2 Outpost (14 / 34). A camp never loses a level, even if you take things down. Some things can only be built once the camp is big enough.

The quickest early points are people and beds: two settlers in two bunks is already 9. After that, a purifier, a guard post, your workshops and a field of crops. Only things on the main map count.

Settlers

Survivors you meet in the Wastes can join you if you have a free Bunk Bed for them, and you can hire a drifter at the Hull for 40 caps. Clear the area first: a frightened survivor will not talk. The ten survivors with tags have their own stories and favours (see People).

Each settler has Farm, Guard and Haul skills and does one job. They get better at it the longer they do it. They eat from your storage chests, so keep food in them. A hungry settler starves, and a starving settler gets hurt.

  • Idle
  • Farmer
  • Guard
  • Hauler
  • Fisher
  • Builder

If a settler drops in a fight, they are knocked out for a day and then come back to their bed. You can change this to permanent death in Settings.

Raids

Raids grow a little as your camp grows, and from Settlement up one of them may bring a heavier fighter. From the second day, raids hit your camp every couple of nights between 19:00 and 23:00. They get worse as the days go on. Walls, gates, guard posts, spike traps and turrets all help, and guards fight back. Each Skull Pike inside your camp weakens raiders by 2%, up to 20%.
