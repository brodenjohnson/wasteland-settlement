Crafting

How crafting grows with your camp, where everything is made, and every recipe.

How it works

Crafting comes in four tiers, and each one opens when your camp is big enough and you have built the station that powers it. Once a tier is open, everything in it can be made at the right bench.

TierNameCamp levelStation to build
1ScavengerFrom the start-
2Forge AgeHamlet camp (34 points)Forge
3SettlementSettlement camp (60 points)Anvil
4IndustryTown camp (140 points)Machine Shop and Generator

Your Progress screen (P) shows the tiers, what each camp level unlocks, and a Next step telling you what to do to open the next one. How camps level up is on Your settlement.

Where things are made

  • Workbench: tools, building parts, furniture and supplies. Works from anywhere inside your camp.
  • Weapon Bench: every weapon and all ammunition. Use it in person: walk up and click it.
  • Armour Bench: every piece of armour. Use it in person.
  • Medical Station: bandages, rad tabs, Rad-Away, medkits, trauma kits and Beast Tonic. Use it in person.
  • Forge, Anvil and Machine Shop: metal plate, steel ingots and machined parts. Building each one also opens its tier. They work from anywhere in camp.
  • Campfire, Stove and Drying Rack: food (see Cooking).

Clicking a Weapon Bench, Armour Bench or Medical Station opens the crafting menu on its own tab. Anything in your storage chests counts toward a recipe while you are in your camp.

Crafting skill

Crafting no longer locks anything. Each level above 1 gives a 4% chance to get half of one ingredient back when you make something.

Unique blueprints

A few items cannot be made until you find their blueprint. Use a blueprint from your bag to learn it for good. The old manuals you may still find (Forge Plans and the like) are read for Crafting experience instead.
