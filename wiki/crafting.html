Crafting

Every recipe in the game, where it is made, what it costs and what unlocks it.

Open crafting with C. Most things are made at a station: the Workbench for tools and early gear, the Forge for metal, the Weapon Bench and Armour Bench for wastelander gear, the Campfire and Stove for food. Benches work from anywhere in your camp zone.

Some recipes need a blueprint. They turn up in ruins and crates, and the Tinkers' Guildhall sells some. Others need a bigger camp or a higher skill. The Crafting skill opens the higher tiers.
