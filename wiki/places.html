Places

The regions of the Wastes, the places under them, and what lives there.

The Wastes are one big map split into regions, each with its own danger and loot. Some places are maps of their own, entered through a door, a hatch or a set of stairs: caves, a mine, a megastore, a relay station, a tunnel, a coast and a town. Leaving a place keeps it as you left it, opened crates and all.

Out in the open you will also find wrecked convoys and raider camps, each with crates and guards. Travellers, the barkeep at the Hull and the Reclaimers sell rumours that mark places on your map.

Places underground are properly dark, and what lives in them is used to it. Go in healthy and fed.

The ways in

  • The Hull: a gangway in the far south-east corner, at the end of the road past the marsh.
  • MegaMart: smashed automatic doors on the far east edge of Fallen City, at the end of the east road.
  • Pylon 3: a service door north of the Ash Tower, at the end of the road through the Ashlands.
  • Deepcut: a mine shaft at the bottom of the quarry road. The Hollow: a cave mouth under the pines. Greybridge Depot: a boarded-up loading bay in Fallen City, once you have a reason to go in.

Regions

The Wastes

Open ground between everything else. Mild radiation, ambushes, and now and then a stranger with something to sell and nothing to lose.

Creatures: Ash Hound, Glowrat

Home Valley

An open valley with a spring and a few trees. Nothing here is safe until you make it so.

Creatures: People, mostly

Whisper Pines

Dense forest. Dark under the canopy, and things wait in it until you are close enough to smell.

Creatures: Ash Hound, Husk, Glowrat, Scrapper

The Ashlands

Grey flats in the north-east where the ash never settles. Ash hounds roam here, and so does what is left of the people who tried to cross.

Creatures: Ash Hound, Husk, Glowrat

Fallen City

Broken towers in the east. Rich crates, raiders, and bodies that have not been touched in years.

Creatures: Scrapper, Gunner, Ash Hound, Husk

Toxic Marsh

Irradiated shallows in the south-east. The water is not for drinking and the mud is not for walking, and people do both.

Creatures: Glowrat, Husk, Ash Hound

The Quarry

Stone and iron ore in the south. Good for materials, and for being buried in.

Creatures: Scrapper, Gunner, Glowrat

The Scrapyard

Heaps of scrap and a few buildings still standing. Somebody lived here recently. Not all of them left.

Creatures: Scrapper, Glowrat, Gunner

The Spine

A service tunnel under the Ash Tower. Dark, cold, and the cable on the wall is humming.

Creatures: Husk, Stalker, Bloater

The Tidewrack

A drowned coast of fog and salt. The tide decides which streets exist.

Creatures: Wrack crab, The Drowned

Greybridge Depot

An old cargo yard run by a man called Varro. Forty people owe him, and there is a cage for the ones who do not.

Creatures: Gunner, Scrapper, Ash Hound

Deepcut

A quarry mine that went under the old riverbed. The shift logs stop at day eleven.

Way in: Mine shaft down to Deepcut. At the end: Hollowclaw Talon

Creatures: Stalker, Husk, Bloater

The Hollow

A cave system under the pines. The dogs went in. The dogs came out.

Way in: A cave mouth under the pines. At the end: Warden's Crossbow

Creatures: Stalker, Glowrat, Spitter

MegaMart

A megastore that kept its doors shut until the event was over. The event is not over.

Way in: Smashed automatic doors. At the end: Loss Prevention Vest

Creatures: Husk, Scrapper, Gunner

Pylon 3

A relay station that carries a broadcast it did not write. The operator’s chair is still warm.

Way in: Service door at the base of the pylon. At the end: Lead-Lined Coat

Creatures: Gunner, Husk, Sniper

The Hull

A container ship that ran aground in the marsh and was never refloated. People moved into the boxes. Then they built a town around it.

Creatures: People, mostly

Tides on the coast

The Tidewrack floods and drains on a twelve-hour cycle: six hours high, six hours low. At high tide the low ground is under water and you cannot cross it. A tide that comes in while you are standing there throws you up the shingle and hurts. A readout shows the tide and when it turns. Sea fog drifts across the whole coast.
