Skills

Eight skills that grow as you use them, and what each level gives you.

Skills level up by doing the thing: chop to get better at gathering, cook to get better at cooking. Check them with P. Your character level is separate. It comes from experience and raises your health and damage.

The tables below show the bonus at a few sample levels.

Chopping, mining and scrounging.
