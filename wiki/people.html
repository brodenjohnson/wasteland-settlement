People

The ten survivors who wear numbered tags, where to find them and what they want from you.

Ten people woke in the ruins around the same time you did, each with a numbered brass tag on their wrist and no clear memory of how it got there. You will find them scattered across the Wastes. Each one has a favour to ask. Help them and they will join your settlement if you have a bed for them, and they get better at their work once their story is done.

What they remember, and what it means, is for you to find out in the game.

Marta Voss

Ran the greenhouse at the old valley co-op until the roof came down. Still carries a packet of seeds in her pocket. Tag 1 is on her wrist and she does not remember putting it there.

Found at: a ruined outpost in The Wastes. Skills: Farm 3, Guard 0, Haul 1

Their favour: The Last Packet. “I have one packet left and it is not enough. Grow me some tomatoes, bring me three, and I will tell you what the label on the case said.”

  1. Grow three Tomatoes
  2. Give Marta three Tomatoes

Reward: 60 XP, Tomato Seeds x6

Dex Harlan

Quarry foreman. Knows every rock in the Scrapyard and holds a grudge against every raider in it. Tag 2. His crew is gone and he will not say how.

Found at: a ruined shed in The Scrapyard. Skills: Farm 0, Guard 2, Haul 3

Their favour: Dex’s Grudge. “Raiders took my crew. Put five of them down and bring me stone for a marker. Ten pieces. Then I will talk.”

  1. Defeat five raiders
  2. Give Dex ten Stone

Reward: 80 XP, Sledgehammer

Wren Alvarez

Scavenger who grew up in the pines. Quiet, quick, and good at not being seen. Tag 3. She woke first and has been walking ever since.

Found at: an old cabin in Whisper Pines. Skills: Farm 1, Guard 1, Haul 3

Their favour: Eyes in the Pines. “I walked the whole map looking for the others. If you want to know what I found, walk it yourself. The pines, the ashlands and the city. Then I will sketch you something.”

  1. Visit Whisper Pines, the Ashlands and Fallen City

Reward: 70 XP, Runner Wraps

Old Tomas

Kept bees before the bombs. Insists the bees survived somewhere. Grows a mean tomato. Tag 4, which he finds hilarious, because he never signed up for anything.

Found at: a hut in Whisper Pines. Skills: Farm 2, Guard 0, Haul 1

Their favour: The Bees Survived. “They survived somewhere. I would bet my boots. Bring me six berries and put a pot of something green on the table. The bees like a garden.”

  1. Give Tomas six Berries
  2. Place a Pot in the camp

Reward: 60 XP, Wild Honey x4

Ripley Cho

Bunker engineer with an engineer’s patience for bad plans. Tag 5. She trusts concrete, steel and very little else.

Found at: a bunker in The Ashlands. Skills: Farm 0, Guard 3, Haul 1

Their favour: Hold the Line. “I will tell you about the bunker. First put two guard towers up and get through a raid. I want to see how you hold a line.”

  1. Build two Guard Posts
  2. Survive a raid

Reward: 110 XP, Riot Helmet, Riot Vest

Sam Oduya

Drove trucks for the depot and knows every road that is still a road. Tag 6. Keeps a spare tyre in his bag, out of habit.

Found at: a ruined shed in The Quarry. Skills: Farm 0, Guard 1, Haul 3

Their favour: Parts and Pieces. “I can fix a truck if you can find me the bits. Twelve scrap and three metal plate. A truck is just a road you carry with you.”

  1. Give Sam twelve Scrap
  2. Give Sam three Metal plate

Reward: 70 XP, Revolver

Lena Fischer

Medic. Steady hands and an unsteady memory. Tag 7. She has stitched herself up twice since waking and is not proud of either.

Found at: a ruined house in Fallen City. Skills: Farm 2, Guard 0, Haul 2

Their favour: Field Clinic. “Give me a clinic and bandages and I will be useful. I would also like to stop stitching myself up.”

  1. Build a Clinic
  2. Give Lena three Bandages

Reward: 90 XP, Medkit x2

Boone Keller

Security. Big, careful, and ashamed of something he will not name. Tag 8. He keeps his back to a wall.

Found at: a ruined house in Fallen City. Skills: Farm 0, Guard 2, Haul 1

Their favour: Dead Man’s Ledger. “There is a Husk walking out there that I think I used to know. Put it down and bring me ten rounds. I will not ask you to do the rest.”

  1. Defeat a Husk
  2. Give Boone ten Pistol rounds

Reward: 100 XP, Rattler SMG

Priya Nair

Radio technician. Can hear a signal through a wall. Tag 9. She does not like the broadcast and will not say why.

Found at: a shack in Toxic Marsh. Skills: Farm 1, Guard 1, Haul 2

Their favour: Dead Air. “The broadcast is not a signal. It is a recording. Get me scrap and two plates so I can build a receiver, then take me to the tower. In spirit. Go and stand under it.”

  1. Give Priya six Scrap
  2. Give Priya two Metal plate
  3. Stand under the Ash Tower

Reward: 120 XP, Geiger Band

Juno Park

The youngest of the ten. Cooked for the wake group. Tag 10. She asks everyone the same question: did you eat?

Found at: a hut in Whisper Pines. Skills: Farm 0, Guard 1, Haul 3

Their favour: Something Warm. “Did you eat? Cook three proper meals, and bring me a stew. Any stew. I want to see if you remember how.”

  1. Cook three meals
  2. Give Juno a stew

Reward: 70 XP, Patchwork Jacket, Wild Honey x2

Other people

Travelling merchants, travellers with rumours, wounded strangers and the occasional drifter all turn up on the roads. Some will join you. Some want something. How you treat them is remembered, and your journal keeps a page of the choices that mattered. The people of the Hull are listed on The Hull page.

Written for version 0.9.3. Numbers on these pages come straight from the game, so they match the build. No story spoilers.
