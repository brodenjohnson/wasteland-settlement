Quests

Early goals, the survivors' favours and Hull jobs. The main story is left out.

Your journal (J) lists every active quest with its next step, and a marker on the screen points the way where it can. You can pin one quest to keep it in view.

The main story starts with the note in your pocket. It is not covered here, so it stays yours to find.

Early goals

These teach the basics and set you up for the long haul.

A Roof and a Fire

The valley is quiet, for now. Get a foothold: a bench to work at, tools in your hands, a bed and a place to keep your things, and food growing in the dirt. Then go and find someone else still alive.

  1. Place your bedroll where you want to live. The ground around it becomes safe
  2. Gather 10 wood and 6 scrap
  3. Build a Workbench (C, Build tab)
  4. Craft a Rusty Hatchet
  5. Craft a Hoe and a Watering Can
  6. Till some soil and plant a seed
  7. Build a Bunk Bed and a Storage Chest
  8. Find a survivor and bring them home

Reward: 60 XP, Canned food x3, Bandage x2

Clean Water

Every drop out here carries something. The valley pond is the cleanest you will find, and a purifier beside it makes it cleaner still.

  1. Unlock the forge (blueprint, or Crafting level 3)
  2. Build a Water Purifier beside the pond
  3. Fill a bottle at the purifier

Reward: 45 XP, Empty Bottle x4

Mouths to Feed

Settlers will eat what is in the chest, and they work better when fed. Fish the pond, and learn what a campfire is for.

  1. Craft a Fishing Rod
  2. Catch a fish
  3. Cook something at a campfire
  4. Watch a settler eat from the chest

Reward: 50 XP, Potato Seeds x4, Tomato Seeds x4

Hold the Gate

Raiders will come through the valley gates. Make them regret it.

  1. Build 8 wall pieces
  2. Build a Gate
  3. Build a Guard Post
  4. Survive a raid

Reward: 90 XP, Pistol rounds x8, Scrap x10

Something to Sell

Merchants wander the wastes. Find one, and learn what your spare scrap is worth.

  1. Meet a travelling merchant
  2. Sell something to them
  3. Buy something from them

Reward: 60 XP, Caps x50

Beyond the Gates

The valley is small. See some of what lies outside it.

  1. Visit 3 different regions
  2. Open a supply crate in the wild

Reward: 70 XP, Rad-Away, Cloth x6

Survivors' favours

Each of the ten survivors has a favour. They are listed with the survivor on the People page.
