Quests

Every quest in the game, with its steps, rewards and how to get unstuck. Story quests are hidden behind spoilers.

Your journal (J) lists every active quest with its next step, and the compass points the way where it can. You can pin one quest to keep it in view. If you are stuck, find the quest below and read the How to note.

Early goals

These teach the basics and set you up for the long haul.

A Roof and a Fire from The Valley

The valley is quiet, for now. Get a foothold: a bench to work at, tools in your hands, a bed and a place to keep your things, and food growing in the dirt. Then go and find someone else still alive.

  1. Place your bedroll where you want to live. The ground around it becomes safe
  2. Gather 10 wood and 6 scrap
  3. Build a Workbench (C, Build tab)
  4. Craft a Rusty Hatchet
  5. Craft a Hoe and a Watering Can
  6. Till some soil and plant a seed
  7. Build a Bunk Bed and a Storage Chest
  8. Find a survivor and bring them home

How to: Put your bedroll down anywhere. Home Valley is the easy choice. The steps tick off as you do them, in any order. Weapons are made at a Weapon Bench, standing next to it. For the last one, find a survivor in the ruins, clear any monsters away from them (a frightened survivor will not talk), and ask them to join you while you have a free Bunk Bed at home.

Reward: 60 XP, Canned food x3, Bandage x2. Leads to: Clean Water, Mouths to Feed, Hold the Gate

Clean Water from Your own throat

Every drop out here carries something. The valley pond is the cleanest you will find, and a purifier beside it makes it cleaner still.

  1. Grow your camp to an Outpost (camp level 2)
  2. Build a Water Purifier beside the pond
  3. Fill a bottle at the purifier

How to: Grow your camp to an Outpost (14 points: see Your settlement), then build a Water Purifier at the Workbench, touching the water's edge. Select an empty bottle and click the purifier.

Reward: 45 XP, Empty Bottle x4

Mouths to Feed from Your settlers

Settlers will eat what is in the chest, and they work better when fed. Fish the pond, and learn what a campfire is for.

  1. Craft a Fishing Rod
  2. Catch a fish
  3. Cook something at a campfire
  4. Watch a settler eat from the chest

How to: Click water with the Fishing Rod, then click again when it bites. Cook the catch at a campfire. Settlers eat from storage chests, so put food in a chest and wait for one to get hungry.

Reward: 50 XP, Potato Seeds x4, Tomato Seeds x4

Hold the Gate from The cliffs

Raiders will come through the valley gates. Make them regret it.

  1. Build 8 wall pieces
  2. Build a Gate
  3. Build a Guard Post
  4. Survive a raid

How to: Raids start on day 2 and come every other night between 19:00 and 23:00. Walls, a gate and a guard post first, then wait for night.

Reward: 90 XP, Pistol rounds x8, Scrap x10

Something to Sell from A stranger on the road

Merchants wander the wastes. Find one, and learn what your spare scrap is worth.

  1. Meet a travelling merchant
  2. Sell something to them
  3. Buy something from them

How to: Travelling merchants turn up by the road from day 2. Talk to one and choose Trade. Sell anything, then buy anything.

Reward: 60 XP, Caps x50

Beyond the Gates from Curiosity

The valley is small. See some of what lies outside it.

  1. Visit 3 different regions
  2. Open a supply crate in the wild

How to: The name of each region shows at the top of the screen as you walk into it. Supply crates sit in ruins, wrecked convoys and raider camps.

Reward: 70 XP, Rad-Away, Cloth x6

Survivors' favours

Each of the ten survivors asks one favour. Finishing it gives you their Tag Card and makes them better at their work. More about each of them is on the People page.

The Last Packet from Marta Voss

Ran the greenhouse at the old valley co-op until the roof came down. Still carries a packet of seeds in her pocket. Tag 1 is on her wrist and she does not remember putting it there.

  1. Grow three Tomatoes
  2. Give Marta three Tomatoes

Reward: 60 XP, Tomato Seeds x6

Dex’s Grudge from Dex Harlan

Quarry foreman. Knows every rock in the Scrapyard and holds a grudge against every raider in it. Tag 2. His crew is gone and he will not say how.

  1. Defeat five raiders
  2. Give Dex ten Stone

Reward: 80 XP, Sledgehammer

Eyes in the Pines from Wren Alvarez

Scavenger who grew up in the pines. Quiet, quick, and good at not being seen. Tag 3. She woke first and has been walking ever since.

  1. Visit Whisper Pines, the Ashlands and Fallen City

Reward: 70 XP, Runner Wraps

The Bees Survived from Old Tomas

Kept bees before the bombs. Insists the bees survived somewhere. Grows a mean tomato. Tag 4, which he finds hilarious, because he never signed up for anything.

  1. Give Tomas six Berries
  2. Place a Pot in the camp

Reward: 60 XP, Wild Honey x4

Hold the Line from Ripley Cho

Bunker engineer with an engineer’s patience for bad plans. Tag 5. She trusts concrete, steel and very little else.

  1. Build two Guard Posts
  2. Survive a raid

Reward: 110 XP, Riot Helmet, Riot Vest

Parts and Pieces from Sam Oduya

Drove trucks for the depot and knows every road that is still a road. Tag 6. Keeps a spare tyre in his bag, out of habit.

  1. Give Sam twelve Scrap
  2. Give Sam three Metal plate

Reward: 70 XP, Revolver

Field Clinic from Lena Fischer

Medic. Steady hands and an unsteady memory. Tag 7. She has stitched herself up twice since waking and is not proud of either.

  1. Build a Clinic
  2. Give Lena three Bandages

Reward: 90 XP, Medkit x2

Dead Man’s Ledger from Boone Keller

Security. Big, careful, and ashamed of something he will not name. Tag 8. He keeps his back to a wall.

  1. Defeat a Husk
  2. Give Boone ten Pistol rounds

Reward: 100 XP, Rattler SMG

Dead Air from Priya Nair

Radio technician. Can hear a signal through a wall. Tag 9. She does not like the broadcast and will not say why.

  1. Give Priya six Scrap
  2. Give Priya two Metal plate
  3. Stand under the Ash Tower

Reward: 120 XP, Geiger Band

Something Warm from Juno Park

The youngest of the ten. Cooked for the wake group. Tag 10. She asks everyone the same question: did you eat?

  1. Cook three meals
  2. Give Juno a stew

Reward: 70 XP, Patchwork Jacket, Wild Honey x2

The Kind Stranger from Mara Quill

A woman needs help digging her brother out of the Greybridge Depot. Her boots are very clean.

  1. Find the Greybridge Depot
  2. Get out of the cage
  3. Get your gear back
  4. Deal with Varro

How to: Mara Quill can turn up by a road from day 2. With Exploring 2 you can look at her boots and see she is lying, call her out, and walk into Greybridge Depot armed and warned. If you go along with her, you are taken and wake in a cage with your gear gone. There is a shiv in the cage: use it on the cage door. Your pack is under a rotten blanket nearby. Varro calls his guards, drops the act at a third of his health, and goes down on one knee near the end. Spare him, take his cache, or finish him. He remembers.

Reward: 240 XP, Rad-Away x2, Pistol rounds x12

Third Drawer from A note in your handwriting

You woke in the valley with nothing but a note in your coat, written in your own hand, by someone who clearly knew a lot more than you do. It points to a depot office in the scrapyard.

  1. Read the note (open your bag, right-click it)
  2. Find the Depot Office in the Scrapyard
  3. Open the crate in the Depot Office
  4. Read the Dispatch Slip

How to: Read the note in your bag first. The Depot Office is a ruin in the Scrapyard, south of the middle of the map. The crate in it holds the Brass Tag and the Dispatch Slip. Read the slip.

Reward: 80 XP, Bandage x2, Canned food x2. Leads to: The Pump House

The Pump House from The Dispatch Slip

Eleven in, eleven out, and a ledger at the pump house in the Toxic Marsh. The water out there is not for drinking, so pack something for the radiation.

  1. Reach the Pump House in the Toxic Marsh
  2. Find the pump ledger
  3. Read the Pump Ledger

How to: The Pump House is in the Toxic Marsh, to the south-east. Take something for radiation (Rad tabs or Rad-Away) and do not drink the water. The ledger is in a crate inside.

Reward: 110 XP, Rad-Away x2, Metal plate x3. Leads to: The Signal

The Signal from The Pump Ledger

Four digits on the back of the brass tag: 4471. The tower in the ashlands still answers on it. Whatever is up there knows who you are.

  1. Reach the bunker under the Ash Tower, in the Ashlands
  2. Open the radio room crate (it wants your brass tag)
  3. Listen to the Signal Tape

How to: The Ash Tower is in the Ashlands, to the north-east. The bunker's radio room crate opens for the Brass Tag in your bag. Listen to the tape by right-clicking it.

Reward: 180 XP, Rad-Away x2, Pistol rounds x10. Leads to: Do Not Take the Stairs

Do Not Take the Stairs from The Pump Ledger

The ledger said to take the tag and leave the stairs alone. The tape said the coast was next. Under the Ash Tower, the stairs go down a very long way.

  1. Take the stairs under the Ash Tower
  2. Restart the three relay panels in the Spine
  3. Open the hatch at the far end

How to: The stairs down are inside the Ash Tower bunker and open once Chapter One is done. The Spine is a long dark tunnel with three relay panels along it; use each one. Husks nest on the way. The hatch at the far end opens once all three relays are running.

Reward: 140 XP, Rad-Away x2, Pistol rounds x8

The Lamplighter from Odile Marr

Odile wants a black case that went down with a supply barge in the Wreck Fields. It can only be reached at low tide.

  1. Recover the Manifest Case from the Wreck Fields at low tide
  2. Bring the case to Odile at Lamp Cove

How to: Odile is in the lighthouse at Lamp Cove, the first place you reach on the coast. She only answers what you ask, so ask everything. The Manifest Case is in the Wreck Fields, which are under water at high tide. The tide readout at the top of the screen says when it turns. Bring the case back to her.

Reward: 200 XP, Bandage x3. Leads to: Quorum

Quorum from Odile Marr

The Cradle will not open for one hand. Six of the eleven tags must be carried to the door.

  1. Carry six Tag Cards
  2. Get past the Listeners at Chapel Row
  3. Read the console at the Cradle door

How to: Each survivor gives you their Tag Card when you finish their favour (see the People page). You need six. There are five ways past the Listeners at Chapel Row: fight them; promise to return the tags (needs Listener reputation 2); lie to them (works with Listener reputation 1, otherwise it becomes a fight); tell them the truth (needs Memory 3, after The Lamplighter); or crawl through the culvert at low tide. The console is at the Breakwater beyond.

Reward: 400 XP, Medkit x2

What Marta Knows from Your own voice

The recording at the Cradle door told you to go home and ask Marta what is in the case. It said she would pretend not to know.

  1. Ask Marta Voss about the case
  2. Open the case

How to: Talk to Marta (at your camp if she joined you, otherwise where you found her) and choose Ask about the case. She will deny it. Memory 4, having been honest with her about the note, trust of 2 or more, or pushing her will all get it out of her. Open it with her, or later by right-clicking it in your bag. If Marta is gone for good, walk to your camp and look under her empty bunk.

Reward: 200 XP. Leads to: Ten Voices

Ten Voices from Dex Harlan, without meaning to

Dex heard nine voices on the radio at night, then ten. Something near you has been answering the broadcast, and whatever follows the broadcast follows the answer.

  1. Ask Priya Nair about the radio

How to: Talk to Priya Nair (at your camp or her shack in the marsh) and choose Ask about the radio. Asking her to stop means fewer Listeners come next. Telling her to keep answering means more.

Reward: 150 XP. Leads to: They Come to Listen

They Come to Listen from The Cantor

The Listeners have heard the answer. They are coming to see who it calls. After that, the Cantor wants a word, in his own chapel.

  1. Be at your camp after dark. They are coming.
  2. Face the Cantor at Chapel Row

How to: Be at your camp in the Wastes between 19:00 and 23:00, at least six hours after talking to Priya. If the Listeners like you (Listener reputation 2, or 1 if Priya went quiet) they come in peace. Otherwise it is a raid. Either way, the Cantor is then waiting in the chapel at Chapel Row on the coast: go down the Ash Tower stairs and through the Spine. You can talk him down with Memory 5, with a valley that can take in 20 or more sleepers (the Long Table box in the settlement panel, B), or with Listener reputation 3. Lying, or a weak answer, starts a boss fight. He calls more Listeners at half health and goes down on one knee near the end.

Reward: 300 XP, Medkit x2. Leads to: The Long Table

The Long Table from The plan in the case

Set a long table at home and sit the ten down. Do the sum again, with them this time.

  1. Build a Table in your camp
  2. Talk to one of the ten and call everyone to the table

How to: A Table needs camp level 2 (Outpost). Build it in your camp, then talk to any of the ten who live with you and choose Call everyone to the table. If none of them live with you, just walk up to the table. The choice you make there carries into Chapter Four.

Reward: 100 XP
