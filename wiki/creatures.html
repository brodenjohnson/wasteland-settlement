Creatures and bosses

What is out there, how hard it hits, where it lives and what it drops.

Enemies are levelled against you. The Level line shows how far above or below your own level each one turns up, and a coloured Lv label over their heads shows how dangerous they are to you. Health and damage below are at their base level and climb a little with each level. Bosses sit well above you and stay there: go in prepared, with armour and healing, or do not go in.

Drop chances are per kill. Human enemies also drop skulls, which go into Skull Pikes and bone gear.
